Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information — including SMS text messaging.

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) applies to data collected by Merchant Credit Card Processing Services, LLC (“MCCPS,” “PayPilot,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) through paypilot.fastapi.online and related PayPilot product pages (the “Website”) and when you communicate with us by phone, email, or SMS text message.

PayPilot is a payment platform offered by MCCPS. By using the Website or opting in to receive messages from us, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Website or provide your phone number for messaging.

Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Contact information such as name, business name, email address, mailing address, and phone number
  • Business details you provide on forms (industry, volume estimates, product interest)
  • Communications you send us, including SMS message content when you text us
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring URL

We collect information you submit on forms (such as Get started), when you call or email us, when you opt in to SMS, and automatically through cookies and similar technologies when you browse the Website.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Set up and service PayPilot accounts and related payment processing
  • Send transactional and account-related notices
  • Send marketing or promotional messages when you have opted in
  • Improve the Website, products, and services
  • Detect fraud, secure our systems, and comply with law

SMS text messaging & opt-in data

If you provide a phone number and consent to SMS, you may receive text messages from PayPilot (MCCPS) about customer care (account setup, support, and service updates) and/or marketing (offers and product information), depending on the boxes you check. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of future messaging. Reply HELP for more information. You can also contact us at 844.826.6227 or legal@mccp.services.

Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services. Consent is provided exclusively for PayPilot / MCCPS to contact you based on your selection, not for any other third parties mentioned on the site.

We do not share data with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Third-party sharing for marketing and promotional purposes excludes text messaging opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties; text messaging opt-in data is not being shared with any third parties.

How we share information

We may share information with service providers who help us operate the Website, deliver messages, process payments, or provide support — only as needed to perform those services and under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share or sell SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies and tracking

We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies for analytics, security, and site functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work if cookies are disabled.

Your choices

  • SMS: Reply STOP to unsubscribe, HELP for help, or START to opt back in where supported.
  • Email marketing: Use the unsubscribe link in marketing emails or contact us.
  • Access / updates: Email legal@mccp.services to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal retention requirements.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards we consider reasonable to protect information under our control. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The updated Policy will be posted on this page. Continued use of the Website or messaging services after changes means you accept the revised Policy.
